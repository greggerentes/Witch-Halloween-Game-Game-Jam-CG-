Cozy Music Lite — 8 seamless music loops x 2 versions, free, for games and videos

A free sample of Cozy Music Complete: one loop from each of 8 cozy music packs, covering farm,
fantasy tavern, spooky Halloween, café, lo-fi, chiptune, RPG battle and zen garden moods.

Every file is an exact number of bars and loops seamlessly: set your audio player to loop and
there is no gap or click. Every track comes in two versions: the full track and a "-calm"
version without the lead melody, made to sit under dialogue and menus. Both have the same
length, arrangement and volume, so you can crossfade between them mid-game.

Format: ogg/  Ogg Vorbis (small, loops perfectly in Godot, Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker, Ren'Py
and the web). 16 files in total.
